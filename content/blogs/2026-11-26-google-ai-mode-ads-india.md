If you run Google Ads in India, a new kind of search result is now showing your competitors' ads, and possibly not yours. Google AI Mode ads have arrived.

On 28 October 2026, Google [started scaling ads in AI Mode](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/) to ten new countries, and India is on the list. AI Mode is the conversational part of Google Search where people ask long, detailed questions and get an AI-written answer instead of ten blue links. Now, when a question has commercial intent, a "Sponsored" ad can appear inside or next to that answer.

For most business owners this raises three questions: does this affect me, do I need to change anything, and is it worth chasing on a small budget? Here is what we are telling our clients.

## Quick answer: what changed for Google Ads in India

Google AI Mode ads come from your **existing** campaigns. There is no new campaign type to create. Search campaigns using broad match (including AI Max for Search), Performance Max and Shopping campaigns are eligible. Exact match keywords are not. Ads are labelled "Sponsored" and appear within or beside the AI answer.

## What AI Mode actually is, and why Indian searchers matter here

AI Mode lets people ask follow-up questions in a chat-like flow. Instead of typing "hotel bhopal", someone asks "quiet hotel near Bhopal railway station with parking, under ₹3,000, good for a family of four".

Google says AI Mode queries are, on average, [about three times longer than traditional searches](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/). That changes how matching works. Short keyword lists struggle with long, specific questions.

India is not a side market for this. When Google brought Search Live to India in October 2025, it called Indians [power users of multimodal search](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/08/googles-search-live-comes-to-india-ai-mode-gets-more-languages/), forming its largest user base for voice and visual search globally, and it expanded AI Mode to seven more Indian languages at the same time. If your customers search in Hindi or by voice, AI Mode is already part of how they find you.

## Which campaigns can show ads in AI Mode

There is no "AI Mode" checkbox in Google Ads. [Search Engine Land's breakdown](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-mode-ads-everything-you-need-to-know-494628) puts it plainly: ads appear through eligible existing campaigns, and turning on AI Max does not let you pick AI Mode as a placement.

Here is how eligibility looks today:

| Campaign setup | Eligible for AI Mode and AI Overviews? |
|---|---|
| Search with broad match keywords and Smart Bidding | Yes |
| Search with AI Max for Search turned on | Yes |
| Performance Max | Yes |
| Shopping | Yes |
| Search with only exact match keywords | No |
| Search with phrase match only | Not listed as eligible by Google |

The exact match point surprises people. Google Ads Liaison Ginny Marvin confirmed that [exact match keywords are not eligible to serve in AI Overviews](https://searchengineland.com/brand-campaign-not-ready-ai-max-479663), even when an identical broad match keyword sits in the same account. Google's [own help page on ads and AI Overviews](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16297775) also says it relies on AI-powered targeting like broad match, AI Max, Performance Max, Shopping and Dynamic Search Ads to match ads to these longer queries.

So if your account is built on tightly controlled exact match keywords, which is common for small Indian advertisers trying to protect a budget, you are simply not in this auction.

## Should you change your setup? A decision table

Do not rebuild your account because of a headline. Use this instead:

| Your situation | What we would do |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 15 to 20 tracked conversions a month | Fix tracking and landing pages first. Broad match without data mostly buys junk clicks. |
| Solid tracking, 30+ conversions a month, exact match only | Test one broad match campaign with Smart Bidding next to your exact match campaign, same landing page, 15 to 20 percent of budget. |
| Already on Performance Max | You are eligible. Check search term insights and add brand exclusions if PMax is eating your brand traffic. |
| E-commerce with a product feed | Make sure Shopping or PMax is live and your feed titles answer real questions (size, material, use case). |
| Lead generation with phone calls | Import offline conversions or at least track calls and WhatsApp clicks, so the system optimises for real enquiries. |

## The 6 account checks to make this week

1. **Confirm conversion tracking is real.** Form submissions, calls and WhatsApp clicks should each be tracked as separate conversions. Google's own [India guide to ads in AI Mode](https://business.google.com/in/resources/articles/guide-to-ads-in-google-ai-mode/) puts accurate conversion tracking first in its list of what helps campaigns perform.
2. **Look at your match types.** If 100 percent of spend is exact match, you are invisible in AI Mode and AI Overviews. Decide whether to test broad match, not whether to switch everything.
3. **Write a proper negative keyword list.** Broad match on long questions will pull in job seekers, students and people looking for free options. Add "job", "salary", "free", "course", "near me jobs" and similar terms that do not fit your business.
4. **Check that your landing pages answer questions, not just sell.** When someone arrives from a long conversational query, a page with prices, inclusions, location and FAQs converts better than a page with a slogan and a form.
5. **Review search terms weekly for the first month.** In AI Max, Google added [a ValueTrack parameter](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-ai-max-for-search-campaigns-39350.html) that returns a keyword-like value for matched queries. Use it, and your search terms report, to see what you are paying for.
6. **Do not chase AI Mode with brand campaigns.** If your brand campaign already works on exact match, leave it. As Search Engine Land notes, adding AI Max to a brand campaign that is already eligible elsewhere [does not add anything new](https://searchengineland.com/brand-campaign-not-ready-ai-max-479663).

## What this means for a small budget

If you spend ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 a month on Google Ads, AI Mode will not change your life this quarter. Most of your results will still come from regular search results and Maps.

What it does change is direction. Google is steadily moving the best placements towards campaigns that let its AI choose queries. Small advertisers who keep running exact match only will slowly see a smaller share of the screen. The safe path is to get your tracking and landing pages ready now, then test broad match carefully once you have enough conversion data.

One more number worth knowing: Google says click-through rates are [already similar for sponsored and organic product listings inside AI Mode](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/). That data is from the US and for product listings, so treat it as a signal, not a promise for a service business in Indore.

## Mistakes we expect to see

- **Switching the whole account to broad match in one day.** Costs jump, lead quality drops, and the owner turns ads off in a panic two weeks later.
- **Letting the Google rep's recommendations auto-apply.** Review each one. Some are useful, some just raise spend.
- **Ignoring the landing page.** A longer, more specific question needs a page that answers it. Thin pages waste the click.
- **Measuring only cost per click.** In AI Mode, fewer but better-qualified clicks can cost more and still be the better deal. Judge on cost per real enquiry.

## What to do this week

- Open Google Ads and check what share of your spend is exact match.
- Make sure calls, forms and WhatsApp clicks are tracked separately.
- Add 20 to 30 negative keywords before any broad match test.
- Pick one service page and add prices or price ranges, inclusions and four FAQs.
- Put a reminder in your calendar to review search terms every Monday for four weeks.
